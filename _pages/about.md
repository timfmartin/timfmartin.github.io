Hey, I'm a passionate robotics AI engineer. Right now I mostly work on reinforcement learning for humanoid locomotion policies in Isaac Lab and Isaac Sim, and on building teleoperation stacks and data pipelines. I'm especially excited about vision-language-action models (VLAs) and world action models (WAMs). I've also worked on bringing frontier LLMs into robotics, for example letting Claude Opus control a robot. I also like tinkering with hardware and PCB design.

Before that I studied mechatronics and information technology at KIT, trained quadruped locomotion policies with reinforcement learning, worked on end-to-end autonomous driving at the Bosch Center for AI, and built a mobile manipulator at ESA's European Astronaut Centre.

This site is where I write about robotics things I build or find interesting. The details are in my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}), and code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/timfmartin).
